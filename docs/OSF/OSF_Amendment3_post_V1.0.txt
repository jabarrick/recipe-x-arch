=== Appended to "Context and additional information" ===
AMENDMENT 3 (submitted 28 November 2026, before Dreyer2023 was downloaded)

Independent test of the cropping effect and of the corrected design.

1. Why. The confirmatory study found that R1 narrowed EEGNet's lead over CSOANet-v2 and that the recipe effect differed across architectures. Post hoc analyses traced these results to two defects in the authors' implementation and one optimisation failure: R1's random crop, applied in training but not at test, harmed EEGNet, ShallowConvNet and MI-BMInet more than CSOANet-v2; the study's ShallowConvNet lacked the reference max-norm constraints; and CSOANet-v2 failed to train in 20 of 30 runs under R3. With the crop removed (R1nc) and the constraints added (ShallowConvNet-MN), no pair of architectures changed definitive pairwise category across recipes, and without R3 neither a recipe effect nor an interaction remained. All of this is post hoc and comes from Lee2019-MI and Cho2017 only. This amendment registers one directional test of the crop effect, and pre-specified secondary analyses of the corrected design, on a dataset that neither author has downloaded or analysed.

2. Data. Dreyer2023 (Dreyer et al., 2023, Sci. Data 10:580, doi:10.1038/s41597-023-02445-z; data at Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.8089820), loaded through MOABB 1.7.2 (moabb.datasets.Dreyer2023, the union of Dreyer2023A, B and C; downloaded by MOABB from its OSF mirror osf.io/8tdk5), left- and right-hand motor-imagery trials only: 87 participants, 27 EEG electrodes at 512 Hz (EOG and EMG channels not used), 240 trials (120 per class) in one session. Exclusions, preprocessing and per-channel gain as in the confirmatory study, with the MOABB default trial window. Cross-subject, 10 folds (split seed 0), 10 % of training subjects for validation, seeds 0, 1 and 2.

3. Factors. Architectures: EEGNet-8,2; ShallowConvNet-MN (the study's ShallowConvNet with the Keras reference max-norm constraints, convolutional filters 2.0 and dense layer 0.5); MI-BMInet; CSOANet-v2. Recipes: R1; R1nc (R1 without the crop, otherwise identical); R2; R4. 4 x 4 x 3 seeds x 10 folds = 480 runs; every trained model is retained.

4. Primary hypothesis HA. Per subject, on seed-averaged float32 balanced accuracy in percentage points: d_A = (EEGNet - CSOANet-v2) under R1nc - (EEGNet - CSOANet-v2) under R1. Predicted d_A > 0 (in the confirmatory data, +4.78 points, 95 % CI 2.66 to 6.88). Test: mean of d_A with a cluster-robust (CR1) standard error over the 10 folds; two-sided p from a wild cluster bootstrap with the null imposed (Webb six-point weights, 9,999 draws, seed 20261126); alpha = 0.05, one hypothesis, no multiplicity correction. Supported: p < 0.05 and positive mean. Contradicted: p < 0.05 and negative mean. Otherwise inconclusive; if the 90 % bootstrap-t interval also lies within +/-2 points (the SESOI), the effect is reported as smaller than the SESOI.

5. Secondary analyses (pre-specified, reported, no decision), on {R1nc, R2, R4} x the four architectures. S1: recipe main effect and recipe x architecture interaction, likelihood-ratio tests with crossed random effects for subject and training run (fold x architecture x recipe), ML, best converged fit over four optimisers, percentage and empirical-logit scales; expectation from the confirmatory data: neither significant. S2: pairwise categories by the registered H3 rule (Bonferroni over 6 pairs x 3 recipes; SESOI 2 points) and the number of definitive category changes across recipes; expectation: none. Also reported: R1nc - R1 per architecture, HA on the empirical-logit scale, and the number of flagged runs under the training-failure rule of Amendment 1.

6. Code. Driver train/independent_dreyer.py (calls the frozen confirmatory driver with the recipe and architecture registrations of train/explore_rev6.py); analysis analysis/independent_analysis.py. Frozen at tag v2.0-independent-frozen, commit 79f8e5a5d5d37680815d3d546d5ae5f651001e27 (github.com/jabarrick/recipe-x-arch), pushed on 28 November 2026 before this amendment was submitted. Dry run on 28 November 2026 on the PhysioNet-MI pilot cache (--pilot-dryrun; 20 subjects, 2 epochs): all 480 runs completed and the analysis script ran on the output; no Dreyer2023 file was downloaded. The full text is docs/OSF/OSF_Amendment3_V1.3.md in that repository.

7. Order. Code frozen; this amendment made public; then Dreyer2023 downloaded, run and analysed. Deviations will be listed in the manuscript's supplementary table S4. A draft of this amendment dated 25 November 2026, never made public, also contained a second hypothesis on R2 and ShallowConvNet; it was withdrawn because the follow-up runs traced that effect to the missing max-norm constraints.
